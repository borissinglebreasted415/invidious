## 🎯 What Is Invidious?

Invidious is a lightweight alternative to YouTube. It lets you watch videos without ads, trackers, or the clutter of the regular YouTube website. You get a clean, fast interface that focuses on the video content itself.

This application runs on your Windows computer. It gives you full control over your viewing experience. No account is required. No personal data is collected.

## ✨ Key Features

**Ad-Free Viewing** – Watch videos without any advertisements. No pre-roll ads, mid-roll ads, or banner ads.

**No Tracking** – Invidious does not track your watch history. It does not save your searches. Your activity stays private.

**Lightweight Design** – The interface loads fast even on older computers. It uses less memory than the standard YouTube site.

**Playlist Support** – Create and manage playlists. Import playlists from YouTube.

**Subscription Management** – Subscribe to channels without creating a Google account. Get updates when new videos are posted.

**Video Downloads** – Download videos in multiple formats and quality levels directly from the application.

**Comment Section** – Read and post comments without needing a YouTube account.

**Dark Mode** – Switch between light and dark themes for comfortable viewing in any environment.

**Proxy Support** – Route traffic through a proxy for additional privacy.

When you open Invidious for the first time, you will see a welcome screen. Follow these steps:

1. Choose your language from the dropdown menu.
2. Select your preferred theme (Light or Dark).
3. Click "Save Settings".

You are now ready to search and watch videos. Type a video title, channel name, or keyword in the search bar at the top.

## 🔍 How to Use Invidious

**Search for videos** – Type your query in the search box and press Enter. Results appear in a clean list format.

**Watch a video** – Click any video result. The video player loads quickly. Use the player controls to play, pause, adjust volume, and toggle fullscreen.

**Download a video** – While watching a video, click the download button below the player. Choose your preferred format and quality. The file saves to your default downloads folder.

**Manage subscriptions** – Click the "Subscriptions" tab on the left sidebar. Enter a channel name to subscribe. Your subscribed channels appear in a feed.

**Create playlists** – Click "Playlists" in the sidebar. Name your playlist. Add videos by clicking the "Add to playlist" button while watching.

## ⚙️ Settings Explained

Open Settings from the gear icon in the top-right corner.

**Appearance** – Change the theme, font size, and layout density.

**Privacy** – Disable or enable watch history. Clear your search history. Choose whether to use a proxy.

**Downloads** – Set default download location. Choose preferred video format and quality.

**Player** – Select default playback speed. Enable or disable autoplay. Set default volume level.

**Advanced** – Configure proxy settings. Set custom instance URL. Reset all settings to defaults.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Invidious does not open** – Make sure your antivirus software is not blocking the application. Add Invidious to your antivirus exclusion list.

**Videos do not load** – Check your internet connection. Restart the application. Try disabling your VPN if you use one.

**Search returns no results** – The search server may be temporarily unavailable. Wait a few minutes and try again.

**Download fails** – Ensure you have enough disk space. Try a different video format. Check your internet connection.

**Application crashes** – Update to the latest version. Restart your computer. Reinstall Invidious if the problem persists.
